Mugen Heartbeat is installed.

No account. No cloud. No subscription. Just your heartbeat.

Next:
1. Start OBS Studio.
2. Add a new source.
3. Choose "Mugen Heartbeat".
4. On Xiaomi Smart Band 10, enable heart-rate sharing before searching for devices.

Installed plugin:
C:\ProgramData\obs-studio\plugins\mugen-heartbeat

Your per-user settings and community skins are stored separately under:
%APPDATA%\obs-studio\plugin_config\mugen-heartbeat

Updating or uninstalling Mugen Heartbeat does not automatically remove that
per-user folder.

Project and releases:
https://github.com/Mugen-Art-Lab/Mugen-Heartbeat
