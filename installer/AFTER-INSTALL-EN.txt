Done! Mugen Heartbeat has been installed.

No account. No cloud. No subscription. Just your heartbeat.

Quick start:
1. Open OBS Studio.
2. If you already have a "Mugen Heartbeat" source, open its properties.
   Otherwise, add a new "Mugen Heartbeat" source.
3. On Xiaomi Smart Band 10, open Settings → Share Heart Rate.
4. In the source properties, click Find devices if your band is not
   selected yet. Select it from the list and click Connect.

Updating the plugin does not require recreating existing sources.
Your OBS scenes and source settings are preserved.

Plugin installation folder:
C:\ProgramData\obs-studio\plugins\mugen-heartbeat

Your settings and user-created skins are stored separately:
%APPDATA%\obs-studio\plugin_config\mugen-heartbeat

Updating or uninstalling the plugin does not delete this user folder.
Custom skins use the skin.json format (schema 1).
Format guide: SKIN-FORMAT.md (in the plugin folder).
